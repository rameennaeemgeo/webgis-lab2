About the Institute

Institute of Geo Information and Earth Observation

The Institute of Geo Information and Earth Observation at PMAS-Arid Agriculture University, Rawalpindi, is an academic and research institution dedicated to the science and application of geospatial technologies. It brings together education, research, and outreach to address environmental, agricultural, and urban challenges through spatial data.

Its programs emphasize Geographic Information Systems, remote sensing, and Earth observation, preparing students to turn satellite imagery and geospatial data into reliable, practical knowledge.
